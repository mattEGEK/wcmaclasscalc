# WCMA Hub: full UX review, 2026-09-30

A review of every screen in the hub for all four roles (member, inspector, media, admin), at phone
width (390px) and desktop width (1366px). Nothing in the app was changed.

## Status: fixed on branch `ux-review-fixes` (2026-09-30)

Every finding below was addressed the same day. The work is on the branch `ux-review-fixes`, not
committed and not pushed. PHPUnit, the JS tests and the phone audit all pass. New behaviour is
covered by `wcma-calculator/tests/UxReviewTest.php` and new steps in the phone audit, which now
also walks the calculator with numbers in, Drivers, Profile, Garage, the Inspector section and the
Media section.

the calculator's boundary gauge and guest nudge, Garage card titles, and links in Inspector tables

## How it was done

- The app ran locally against a throwaway seeded database. The real database was never touched.
- A crawler followed every link for each role and took a full-page screenshot at both widths
  (about 190 pages).
- A second pass drove the states a crawl can't reach: sign-in and sign-up errors, the phone menu,
  an empty tech sheet submit, the checklist, the calculator with numbers in, the feedback box, admin
  edit dialogs, car and gear photo uploads and submits, the inspector's photo review, accepting and
  sending back a class declaration, print views, a tablet width, and keyboard focus order.
- The code was read where a screenshot raised a question.

**Not covered:** real phones, screen readers, the ice and TA/Drift sheet forms beyond what the phone
audit already checks, HTML email rendering (only the plain text was read), and the photo lightbox
(the test click did not open it, so it is unverified either way). The seed data has one member with
two cars, so long lists were not seen.

## What already works well

- The numbered "things to do before [event]" list on Home. It is the best screen in the app.
- Garage cards: number roundel, class badge, one status per line, one clear next action.
- Keyboard focus is always visible (3px red outline), there is a skip link, and tap targets are 44px.
- Admin list + edit dialog pattern (Users, Events, Clubs). "Did it save?" is answered by a flash.
- Photo pre-tech: progress bar, per-photo status, plain instructions per photo.
- The tech sheet keeps a draft across reloads, and one signature covers entrant and driver.
- Emails are short, say what happened, and link to the right page.
- The printed tech sheet is clean and fills the page.

## High priority

### H1. The desktop header wraps for staff at every width, and for members on tablets
With Inspector, Media and Admin in the nav, "Class Calculator" breaks onto two lines and "Profile ·
Sign out" drops under the name. It happens at 1024, 1180, 1280, 1366 and 1440px, because the row is
capped at 1180px. Members see the same break at about 820px (tablet).
**Fix:** rename the item "Calculator", put Profile and Sign out behind the name, and switch to the
Menu button below about 1000px instead of 700px.

### H2. The tech sheet reveals problems one at a time, in a jumpy order
Submitting an empty sheet shows only "Choose Yes or No for the log book" near the bottom. The next
submit jumps up to the checklist. Then come Driver 1, equipment, and signatures. A member can need
five attempts to learn everything that is missing.
**Fix:** check everything on one submit, show a short list at the top ("4 things to finish") that
links to each one, and focus the first problem in page order.

### H3. Sent-back work disappears from Home when no event is tagged
The to-do list is built only for events the member has said they are going to
(`readiness-lib.php`, `buildReadiness`). A class declaration that an inspector sent back then shows
only as a small red chip in "At a glance", below every event card. The same applies to photos sent
back for a retake. The member does get an email.
**Fix:** a "Needs your attention" block at the top of Home that does not depend on events.

### H4. Home is very long and repetitive on a phone
With five upcoming events Home is about 4,500px tall. Every event card is an open form: car picker,
Running checkboxes, the supplementary-regulations tick, a reminder checkbox and a button. "Email me
reminders for events I'm going to" is one account setting but appears on every card. On a card where
one car is already going, the form for adding a second car sits directly under it with no heading,
so it reads as part of the first entry.
**Fix:** show each event as one row (name, date, "I'm going"). Open the choices only after the tap.
Ask about reminders once. Label the second form "Add another car".

### H5. The inspector's photo review is a single 9,000px column on desktop
Fifteen photos are stacked one per row at 334px wide with the right two-thirds of the screen empty.
Every photo has an always-open "What is wrong with this photo?" box. "Accept photos" and "Send back
for retakes" are at the very bottom. A different red button, "Accept — teched in person", with a
signature pad, sits at the top of the same page, so the first accept an inspector meets is the wrong
one for a photo review.
**Fix:** three photos per row on desktop, show the note box only when "Needs a retake" is ticked,
keep the two photo actions in a bar that stays in view, and fold the in-person accept away when the
page is opened from the review queue.

### H6. Two visual systems are still in play
- The tech sheet form, gear pre-tech, photo pre-tech and the inspector's sheet and gear pages use the
  older boxed page: the header and stripe are inset, section headings are small and underlined, and
  titles are in Title Case. Moving from Garage to a tech sheet shifts the whole header sideways.
- The old palette leaks through: navy table headers, a light-blue info box on the calculator, and
  light-blue links ("Review" in the Gear table, "Mark all OK") at about 3.1:1 contrast, under the
  4.5:1 the phone audit enforces elsewhere.
- Links come in three colours: browser-default blue (Profile, Sign out, "Open ↗", "Edit media
  profile", Inspector back links), brand red (member back links, "Not going anymore"), and grey
  (footer).
**Fix:** one link colour, one page shell, and retire the old colour variables in `calculator.css`.

## Medium priority

### M1. A new member's first screen does not say where to start
Home opens with two headings back to back ("Which events are you going to?", "Upcoming events") and
five "Add a car for this event" buttons. "Start by adding your car" is further down. The signed-out
landing page makes Class Calculator the main button and does not say what the hub asks of a member.
**Fix:** a three-step start (add your car, declare its class, say which events), shown until done.

### M2. The calculator hides its answer on a phone
- The result panel is below the Submit button, so the class is out of sight while choosing
  modifiers. A small class readout that stays in view would fix this.
- Contact details and documents come before the numbers.
- "Print out a copy for yourself before hitting submit" is out of date: the hub emails a copy.
- Errors here are browser pop-up bubbles; the rest of the hub uses inline messages.
- "Save to My Cars" and "Load Saved" do not match "Garage" used everywhere else.
- The "--" boxes under Weight and HP have no label.
- The page has no main heading.

### M3. Inspector filters take half the first screen on desktop
Event roster, Classing and Gear stack every filter full width. On Classing the "Class" label sits
alone beside the search box. The admin Users tab already has the right pattern: one inline row.

### M4. Inspector pages are titled by database id
"Tech Sheet #1" and "Gear #1" mean nothing at the track. Use "#17 Mazda Miata — Fall Sprint" and
"Jordan Lee — 2026 gear".

### M5. "Not going anymore" acts at once
There is no confirm and no undo, and the Running and driver choices are lost. Archiving a car does
ask first. Add a confirm, or an Undo in the flash message.

### M6. Status wording and colour are not consistent
- One state has five names: "With an inspector", "Submitted", "Pending", "Photos pending review",
  "waiting for review".
- A submitted sheet shows "Car status: Needs tech at the track" at the top and "Status: Submitted —
  awaiting review" at the bottom.
- The "your photos were submitted" confirmation is an amber pill, which reads as a warning.
- "Photo needed" (required) and "No photo yet" (optional) are the same amber.
**Fix:** one table of states with one phrase and one colour each; grey for optional.

### M7. The car page has competing main buttons
"Re-declare class" is red even when the class is accepted or already with an inspector, which
invites needless re-declaring. It shares the page with two more red buttons. Its buttons are
centred; every other card is left aligned. "Earlier tech sheets" lists the sheet for an upcoming
event.

### M8. Fields stretch across the desktop, and buttons touch the field above
A licence number or co-driver name gets a 1,100px box. On Create Account, Profile, Add a co-driver
and Feedback the button sits flush against the last field. Cap field width and give every field the
same bottom gap.

### M9. The Media section is a step behind the rest
Labels sit beside inputs instead of above, some inputs have the browser's default border, "Send
back" wraps inside a narrow button next to a "Hide everywhere" link, and the event picker shows
"2026-10-16". On the Announcer sheet each entry repeats the car line twice.

### M10. Dates appear in eight formats
"Fri, Oct 16", "Oct 16", "Oct 16, 2026", "October 16, 2026", "Friday, October 16", "2026-10-16" and
more. Pick one short and one long form.

### M11. The media profile shows twelve empty sponsor fields
Show one sponsor and an "Add another sponsor" button.

### M12. System gaps are shown to members instead of admins
Members read "This event has no host club yet. Ask an admin." beside greyed checkboxes that are hard
to see. The admin Events list shows a dash for the missing club but does not flag it, and lists the
furthest event first.
**Fix:** flag events with no host club in the admin list, sort soonest first, and hide the disabled
choices from members.

### M13. Three ways to mark required, two heading styles
Fields use "(required)", "(optional)" and red asterisks. Headings mix "Submit Tech Sheet", "Create
Account" and "Sign In" with sentence case everywhere else.

### M14. Driver equipment on the tech sheet
The Suit rating box suggests "SA2020", which is a helmet standard. The hint text is the only label
for both rating boxes. "Confirm" does not say what is being confirmed ("I have this" would).

## Low priority

- **L1. Create Account** shows one error per attempt, does not state the 8-character rule up front,
  and has no Google option although Sign In does.
- **L2. Drivers page:** the licence "Save" is a text link; the gear chip sits in a different place on
  your card and a co-driver's; "Add photos" does not say gear.
- **L3. Add a car:** the four "Where will this car race?" tiles are uneven, and the MotorsportReg
  note about car numbers is at the bottom instead of under Car number.
- **L4. Opening gear photos creates a record.** Following the "Add photos" link creates the season's
  gear record, so drivers who only looked appear in the inspector's Gear list.
- **L5. Season links** edits in the table row while other admin tabs use a dialog. "From
  MotorsportReg" on Events is a bare link in a card with no count or explanation.
- **L6. Status chips wrap badly on phones:** "Gear tech:" and "Needs gear check at the track" split
  into two columns inside the pill.
- **L7. Declaration review:** the calculation is in a typewriter font, and the send-back note is
  always open under Accept.
- **L8. Spacing:** card headings touch the chip or text below them; "At a glance" touches its cards;
  a flash message touches the back link under it.
- **L9. Tech sheet view on screen:** each checklist table is a different width. Print is fine.
- **L10. Profile** has no main heading.
- **L11. Roster wording:** "has a sheet, not tagged" is internal language.
- **L12. Radio and checkbox labels** sit a few pixels below the control (already known).
